# Orbital Station artwork

Both original illustrations were created with the built-in `imagegen` tool on
2026-10-30. The planet background and transparent station sprite are bundled
unchanged in both targets. At runtime all animation runs locally in Swift;
no API key, network service or image generation is needed.

- `orbit-planet.png`: original terrestrial world, atmospheric limb, terminator,
  clouds and night-side lights against a starfield.
- `orbit-station.png`: original detailed wheel habitat and docking hub with
  true alpha transparency, radiator/solar wings and inhabited modules.

No game screenshots, film graphics, spacecraft models or other third-party
assets were used. Native rendering adds attitude changes, rotating ring traffic,
ships, docking lights and a moving orbital day/night veil.

## Planet background — exact prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original full landscape 16:10 background for an animated native macOS space-station screensaver. Finished high-quality painterly pixel-art cosmic environment, tiny deliberate pixel clusters and intricate natural texture, mature cinematic science-fiction indie game aesthetic, not a flat vector illustration, no UI. Scene: an enormous blue terrestrial planet dominates the LEFT half to two thirds of the picture. Its curved globe and luminous thin blue atmosphere fill the left foreground and lower-left; some of its lower edge may extend past the bottom frame. The planet has textured swirling clouds, deep navy oceans and rugged coastlines, sunlight from upper left, a broad twilight terminator toward its right and night side with a restrained web of tiny warm amber city lights. No recognisable exact Earth geography is required; an original habitable world. A delicate atmospheric halo at the curved limb, beautiful blue/steel/indigo palette with subtle warm lights. The RIGHT half of the frame is calm deep near-black star field with sparse crisp stars and only a faint distant dusty blue nebula. Leave that right half mostly empty for an animated orbital station and ships to be drawn in code, no intense bright objects there. The composition should communicate vast scale, quiet human habitation and peaceful orbital night. NO spacecraft, stations, ships, satellites, astronauts, planets other than the main globe, foreground objects, text, logos, numbers, border, watermark or HUD. No exaggerated bloom or lens flares, no colourful fantasy nebula overload, no photorealistic camera grain. Atmospheric and finely detailed, with strong depth and coherent intentional pixel-art material.
```

## Station sprite — exact prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE detailed original orbital space station sprite, genuine transparent alpha background around the whole station and through openings. Primary subject: a large believable orbital settlement in three-quarter view, a monumental rotating wheel habitat around a central spindle, four structural spokes, an elongated central docking hub, complex trusses, pressure modules, radiator fins and several broad dark blue/copper solar-panel wings. Rounded habitat ring but strong industrial engineering, many tiny warm amber windows, muted cool cyan navigation lights, intricate pale ivory and graphite machinery, age and subtle wear. Several clearly visible docking arms extending from the lower-right and lower-left sides of the hub, with small hangar entrances. Full complete station with comfortable 7% transparent margin; all solar arrays, hub and arms must fit in image. The wheel is viewed obliquely as a broad ellipse, upper-left rim lit by cold sunlight, right and underside in deep shadow. Detailed painterly pixel art with crisp small pixel clusters, richly textured panels, convincing materials and real scale like a finished mature science-fiction indie game asset. Perspective and lighting must be coherent, not a toy, not a flat icon, not a glossy 3D product render, not a simple set of rectangles. Original design, no existing film/game spacecraft design or branding. Composition roughly square or 4:3, hub at centre, ring across central two-thirds, solar wings spread diagonally outward but not overwhelming station. NO planet, stars, cosmos, background, backdrop, cast shadow outside object, particles, exhaust, ships, astronauts, text, logos, labels, border or UI. True transparency surrounding and between every structural component. Calm elegant industrial architecture in orbit.
```
